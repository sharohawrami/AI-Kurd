Building the research capacity to make advanced AI safe, starting in Kurdistan.

We introduce students and young professionals to AI safety: how modern AI is changing the world, what can go wrong, and how they can build careers that help. Small cohorts, open discussion, and a direct line to the global AI safety community.

Why the Hub exists

Talent is everywhere. Access to this field is not.

Work on making AI systems safe is concentrated in a handful of cities. Students in Erbil, Sulaimani, Duhok and Halabja have the talent to take part, but few structured routes in: no local community, no mentors, and no one to ask where to start.

The Hub is that route, built to the standard of established international programs and grounded in our own context.

After 12 weeks

What you’ll be able to do

Understand it

Explain in plain words how today’s AI systems are made, what they do well, and where they fail.

Think it through

Weigh the main arguments about AI risk, and say which you find convincing and why.

Present yourself

Speak and write clearly, work well in a team, and have a CV and profile ready for opportunities.

Take the next step

Leave with a personal plan for your next six months: a program to apply to, a skill to build, or a person to learn from.

Our program draws on openly published material from established AI safety programs. We are not formally affiliated with those organizations.
