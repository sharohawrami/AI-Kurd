01 / ALIGNMENT

What is AI Alignment?

هاوڕێککردنی زیرەکیی دەستکرد چییە؟

Alignment is the challenge of making AI systems pursue the goals we intend — not just the goals we literally specify. A system optimizing the wrong objective can cause harm even without any bad intent. As models grow more capable, getting this right becomes one of the most important technical problems of our time.

02 / INTERPRETABILITY

Looking Inside the Black Box

سەیرکردنی ناوەوەی سندوقە ڕەشەکە

Interpretability research tries to understand why a neural network produces the answers it does. If we can read a model's internal reasoning, we can catch deception, bias, and errors before they matter — like an MRI for artificial minds.

03 / EVALUATION

Testing Frontier Models

تاقیکردنەوەی مۆدێلە پێشکەوتووەکان

Before deployment, frontier models are stress-tested for dangerous capabilities and failure modes — red-teaming, benchmark evals, and safety cases that must hold up under adversarial pressure.

04 / GOVERNANCE

Policy & Responsible Scaling

سیاسەت و گەشەپێدانی بەرپرسانە

Technical safety needs institutional support: scaling policies, model audits, and international coordination so that safety keeps pace with capability everywhere — including our region.
