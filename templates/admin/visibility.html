Show and hide

Hide what isn’t ready yet

Hidden pages and sections disappear for visitors, in English and Kurdish, and come back the moment you show them again. Nothing is deleted, and you can still see and edit them yourself.

You can also hide sections straight on a page: open it with Edit this page and use the Hide button in the corner of each section.

Whole pages

Hiding a page also removes its links from the menu and the footer. Visitors who open its address see “Page not found”.
